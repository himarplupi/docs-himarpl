Hey there! 👋 Let's talk about some important things to keep in mind for future HIMARPL cabinets when they're building their own version of the organization's website.

## What We Learned & Suggest

- **Revive the old projects!** Right now, our old projects aren't being used. They're great projects, but in this cabinet, we aren't developed them. If possible, future cabinets could revive the old projects such as bot-himarpl and blog-himarpl. However, this can be discussed with the next Head of the KOMINFO Department. If they're no longer relevant, it's okay to abandon them. Oh, and lastly, lomba-himarpl can also be revived since it's a light project.

## Tips for Future Teams

- **Keep What Works:** Feel free to stick with our basic website structure - home page, about page, news, etc. It's a solid foundation!

- **Make It Your Own:** While the current layout is good, don't be afraid to try different tools or technologies that might work better for you. Innovation is always welcome! 

- **Don't Forget the Footer:** One must-have is the global footer component - it helps users navigate between all the HIMARPL website pages easily. Think of it as the glue that holds everything together! 🔗
